# Returns and Refunds Policy

**Version:** [VERSION] · **Effective from:** [DATE]

## 1. Return window

- Each product page shows its return window. It is counted from the day the order is delivered.
- *The software allows 0 to 90 days per product; the default is 7 days. A window of 0 means the item cannot be
  returned. If an order has several items, the shortest window among them applies to the order.*
- You can request a return only after the order has been delivered and before the window closes.

## 2. Items that cannot be returned

- **Personalised items**, made with your text or a design you chose. We tell you this before you add the item to
  your cart and again at checkout.
- **Food and other items marked non-returnable** on the product page, for hygiene or safety reasons.
- *Current software behaviour: if an order contains any non-returnable item, the whole order cannot be returned
  online. Counsel must confirm this is acceptable; open question 24.* If your order contains such an item and
  something arrived damaged or wrong, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will [REMEDY, per counsel].

## 3. How to request a return

1. Open your order from your account, or from the link in your order confirmation.
2. Choose **Request a return** and pick a reason: damaged, wrong item, not as described, changed my mind, or other.
   You can add a short note.
3. We will [PICKUP OR DROP-OFF ARRANGEMENTS] within [PERIOD].

Returned items must be [CONDITION REQUIREMENTS: unused, original packaging, tags, accessories].

## 5. Refunds

- We refund after we receive and check the returned item.
- Refunds go to your **original payment method**:
  - Card, net banking or UPI through our payment provider: refunded through the provider. [TIMELINE.]
  - [DIRECT UPI, if used]: refunded by UPI transfer to the account you paid from. [TIMELINE.]

  *Gateway refunds are requested automatically. Direct-UPI and COD refunds are manual: the store records them and a
  person sends the money.*

You can cancel from your order page until the order ships. If you have paid, we refund the full amount to your
original payment method. COD orders need no refund.
